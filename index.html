The problem

  • Cut through aluminum angle on Mars
  • Powered only by an air compressor
  • Mostly 3D printed parts
  • Parts swap out easily at any time
  • Easy to use with thick space gloves

Meet the team

We're a group of young engineers at Harlingen Collegiate High, working to solve a problem for future astronauts. Our tool cuts aluminum on Mars using only compressed air, and every part is built to be swapped out and used with thick gloves. We design it, test it, and keep improving it.

Teachers: Mercedes Espinoza, Mr. Oviedo · NASA HUNCH project: Pneumatic Tools
